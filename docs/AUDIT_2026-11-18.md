# Audit — Saltwharf vertical slice (M1–M7), 18 Nov 2026

Audited against `docs/GAME_DEV_PLAYBOOK.md`. Build `09d5d30` as deployed to GitHub Pages.

**Verdict:** the systems work and hold together, but the slice is not yet fun for long. Within a week of play the player has spare money with nothing worth buying, NPCs who never change how they treat you, and no reason to look forward to tomorrow. The phone UI also crowds the screen and makes people hard to tap. Most of the fixes are content and small rules changes, not new systems.

Other defects found while reviewing:

- Tapping open water or a spot with no walkable tile nearby does nothing at all, with no feedback.
- `?debug` and `?mgseconds` work on the live site. Harmless for a prototype; remove before release.
- Residents can stand on the tile in front of a door and on top of each other. They don't block you, but it looks wrong.
- A robbery can fire while you are walking toward a building; the building's panel then opens after the fight.

## What's working well

- **Tone.** Pembleton, Grubb, Rudd and Bessa have voices; the writing carries the gothic-dry mood the Story Bible asked for.
- **The day has shape.** Dawn shift, free afternoon, evening shift, dangerous night, rent at month's end: the Sims/Stardew rhythm is there.
- **Failure is survivable.** Debt, eviction, hospital and firing all hurt but never end the game.
- **Cargo Stacking** is a genuinely skill-based little game with a clear risk (lean) and reward (heavy crates).
- **Saltwharf reads as a place.** Strong river edge, a central street, a market node and a distinctive Vane Lamp.
