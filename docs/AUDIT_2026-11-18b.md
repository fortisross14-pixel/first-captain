# Audit: Saltwharf and Act I (M1–M17), 18 Nov 2026 (second pass)

Audited against `docs/GAME_DEV_PLAYBOOK.md`, before moving on to new districts. Build after `da6d19b` (M17), local preview, iPhone 13 emulation.

**Verdict:** Saltwharf is close to finished as a first chapter. The story, side stories, homes, decor, scavenging, saves and maps are in. What's missing is mostly in four places:
- three of the six skills (Study, Arts & Crafts, Occult) still can't earn a living;
- the church, a story location, can't be entered;
- newer systems (map, Decor, scavenging, story bubbles) are never introduced;
- the interface has grown back over its size budget.

One bug was found and fixed during the audit: the clock kept running under the prologue, place panels and Decor mode.

## What's working well

- **Act I has shape**: five chapters with real choices; every locked step tells you exactly what you're missing.
- **The side stories use the town**: the yard, the pump, the Wherry Stairs and the Cup all matter now.
- **The journal** is the reference a life-sim needs: quests, requirements, job hours and promotion in one place.
- **Saltwharf is lived in**: people keep near home, drink inside, go to church; the street is never crowded.
- **The prologue** sets the tone before the first tap.
